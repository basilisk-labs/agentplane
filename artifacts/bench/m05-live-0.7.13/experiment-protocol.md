## Treatments and common workflow

For each matched task, keep product build, starting repository, objective, permitted paths/effects, policy, provider/model/effort, runtime, transport, cache/session policy, mandatory checks and final oracle identical. Only Recipe treatment differs.

- `no_recipe`: give the ordinary native planner the objective and repository evidence. Count preparation of its actual Plan; do not provide a free answer Plan.
- `instantiate`: select an exact pinned applicable Recipe with complete typed bindings. Count selection, applicability observation, retained closure, compilation and any required ordinary planning.
- `specialize`: request genuine semantic specialization through native preparation and PLANNER admission. Count all specialization work. Never replace a draft with a known expected Plan or patch.

Each arm must follow the same existing native approval, scoped execution, deterministic verification and independent EVALUATOR obligations until the registered endpoint. Record emitted episodes and their exact result/resume bindings. A benchmark actor must not fabricate USER receipts, substitute a terminal state, omit review or weaken checks. Capture every role, including selection, planning, execution, review, orchestration and any recovery role actually invoked.

Use isolated fresh task repositories for each assignment. Recipe guidance may describe reusable strategy, not embed expected patches or hidden test answers. Freeze the operator intervention policy before assignment; count all interventions and waiting. The verified-result endpoint requires native verification, independent review and the external study oracle. Report native closure separately. Whether branch closure includes a real hosted provider or ends at local verified readiness is an unresolved campaign endpoint choice that must be fixed and symmetric before launch; do not claim hosted closure if it is excluded.

## Pilot registration and assignments

The minimum pilot is **5 tasks × 3 arms × 5 matched repetitions = 75 assignments**. One task per stratum is a tooling and variance pilot, not five independent tasks in each stratum. Repetitions measure within-task variability; they do not increase the independent task-cluster count. Five task families provide very few independent clusters. More repetitions of those same tasks do not establish broad statistical power. Bound any conclusion to the tested corpus and registered task population; do not generalize to untested workflows.

Register the complete assignment list, seed, task/arm/repetition identities and execution order before any pilot call. For each task use five distinct permutations of the three arms, selected by a frozen seed from the six possible orders; randomize their repetition order. This gives near-balanced arm positions for five repetitions. Randomize task block order and retain the omitted permutation. Larger repetition counts use balanced complete blocks with any remainder rule fixed in advance. Keep concurrency and cache/session resets identical and recorded.

Assign budget reservations before calls. Preserve failed, cancelled, interrupted, blocked and never-started assignments in the ledger. Never silently replace a failed attempt, run a favorable extra repetition or remove an unmatched failure. Retry counts and per-call/campaign limits must be numeric and authorized before launch. Stop on exhausted authority or budget; unfinished assignments remain unfinished, not zero-cost successes.

Pilot goals are workflow/oracle validation, accounting completeness, feasibility and variability estimation. Publish its full descriptive results, including adverse findings. Pilot data cannot establish the confirmatory release claim.

## Independent confirmation registration

After the pilot is closed, register a separate fixed confirmation campaign before observing any confirmation outcomes. Use new task instances from a previously specified task population, not pilot tasks selected for favorable Recipe performance. Prefer independently authored tasks and hold their oracle outputs from treatment agents. Freeze selection/exclusion rules based on task validity and scope, not observed treatment cost or success.

Use pilot cluster-level variability and failure rates to plan confirmation precision and power under the proposed minimum effect and quality margins. Evaluate a conservative sensitivity range rather than using only the most favorable pilot estimate. A statistician or independent reviewer must approve the prospective calculation and exact number of independent tasks per stratum, repetitions, allocation, fixed budget and analysis code. These numbers are unresolved; no live confirmation may begin until they are recorded. If feasible budget cannot support the required precision, report that limitation instead of reducing the success standard.

Do not combine pilot data with confirmation estimates. Revisions discovered in the pilot create a new version with new hashes. Any product, oracle, treatment or eligibility change during confirmation invalidates that registered analysis; retain all accrued data and require a new prospective study. Do not extend a sample after inspecting significance. Administrative or safety stops are allowed, but produce a stopped/inconclusive record rather than proof.

## Accounting and estimands

For arm a, the primary cost estimand is:

`sum(observed cost of every assigned attempt in a) / independently verified coding successes in a`.

Count all provider calls and billable failures, including selection, inline Plan construction, specialization, retries, independent native review and host orchestration. Distinguish semantic episodes, dispatches, provider turns and tool calls. Retain requested and observed model/effort. Monetary claims require a pinned applicable price basis or actual attributable billed cost; tokens alone do not prove money savings. Cached input is a subset of input, and reasoning is a subset of output; do not add either twice.

Report all-assigned success rates, absolute totals and costs per success. Zero successes yield no finite cost-per-success estimate. Missing, partial or unattributable usage remains unknown; known cost is only a lower bound. A missing receipt is not a free call. Do not calculate a complete comparative claim unless every material cost is observed or a defensible preregistered bound resolves the decision. Preserve unknown effects and reconcile through supported evidence, never relaunch uncertain work to obtain a cleaner record.

Report setup-inclusive and steady-state costs separately. Recipe authoring, installation, retention and study preparation must be attributed transparently. Common study-only oracle development/execution is reported separately from deployable task cost, using the same rule for all arms. Native independent review remains in task cost. Charge one-time setup once, disclose allocation, and report a break-even reuse count with uncertainty; do not assume free setup or unlimited future reuse. Exact allocation and plausible reuse horizon require ratification before confirmation.

Record intent-to-first-scoped-mutation, intent-to-verified-result and intent-to-native-closure separately. Partition preparation, provider, checks, Git/filesystem, USER waiting and external waiting into nonoverlapping spans. Report active and elapsed time, censoring/unfinished attempts and failure rate together; successful-only speed summaries are secondary and cannot hide failures.

## Analysis and proposed decision thresholds

Analyze independent task clusters, carrying all repetitions and matched arms together during paired resampling. Stratify by workflow, transport and cache, and preserve the frozen stratum weights. Estimate cost-per-success ratios and differences, success-rate differences and time distributions. Matched pairs in which both arms succeeded are secondary diagnostics; primary accounting retains everyone. A bootstrap resample with zero successes has no finite ratio and cannot be dropped to obtain a favorable interval.

Proposed confirmation settings below require explicit ratification before registration. They are not an approval or observed result:

- Two treatment-versus-`no_recipe` primary comparisons, with familywise error controlled at 0.05. Use a prespecified Bonferroni one-sided 97.5% upper bound for each cost ratio; retain dependence by resampling matched task clusters. Freeze the resampling algorithm, seed, count and treatment of nonfinite samples.
- Proposed practical minimum saving: 5%, requiring each claimed treatment's cost-ratio upper bound below 0.95. The exact economic margin is unresolved until ratified; do not confuse the charter's suggested regression tolerance with proven savings.
- Require no observed forbidden-effect, authority, oracle-tampering or review-bypass violation. Any such violation stops the campaign and defeats a safety-equivalent benefit claim. Zero observed violations is not proof of universal safety.
- For a tested-corpus claim, require paired oracle-equivalent outcomes and no observed quality regressions, while reporting success-rate uncertainty explicitly. This exact observed-corpus gate is not a population noninferiority guarantee. A zero-point population margin with nonzero-width uncertainty can be unattainable even when all observed pairs agree. Any population quality noninferiority margin and simultaneous coverage method remain unresolved and require prospective independent ratification; do not weaken Q00 or infer population equivalence from matching small counts.
- Proposed active verified-result time noninferiority margin is 5%. Missing/censored times and all-assigned success must remain visible. Ratify the endpoint, margin and joint gate before confirmation. A cost gain with unacceptable quality/time loss is not a net benefit.

All required gates form an intersection for a claimed treatment. Do not choose which arm, stratum, endpoint or margin to claim after seeing confirmation results. Register treatment-specific claims and any aggregate decision in advance. Negative-control safety results remain mandatory even if the claimed gain concerns only exact-match tasks. The offline NDWDC5 percentile implementation is descriptive tooling; it does not by itself qualify these confirmation inference procedures.
